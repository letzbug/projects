AuraFrame

An interactive AI-powered UniPop information kiosk combining a locally running language model with voice interaction, visual course information and dedicated hardware.

CURRENTLY IN DEVELOPMENT

Overview

AuraFrame is an ongoing UniPop project that explores how a local artificial-intelligence system can transform a traditional information screen into an interactive digital assistant.

The concept is designed around a physical freestanding kiosk equipped with a display, camera, microphone and speakers. Instead of simply showing course advertisements, the system allows visitors to interact directly with the information using natural spoken questions.

AuraFrame can present a UniPop course visually and at the same time answer questions such as what the course is about, when it takes place, where it is organised or what other relevant courses are available.

Project details

The core idea behind AuraFrame is to move beyond passive digital signage and create a system that can actively help visitors discover the UniPop course programme.

A local language model is being prepared specifically around UniPop information and course content. This allows the assistant to understand questions about the programme and provide answers based on the information available to the system.

A visitor might see a course on the display and ask: “What is this course about?” or “Where does it take place?” AuraFrame processes the spoken question, retrieves the relevant course information and responds through the speakers while simultaneously presenting supporting information and images on the display.

The intention is to create an interaction that feels more like speaking with an information assistant than navigating a conventional touchscreen menu.

Local AI

The language model runs locally and is being adapted to understand UniPop-specific information and course content.

Natural voice interaction

Visitors can ask questions directly using normal spoken language instead of navigating through menus and buttons.

Course intelligence

AuraFrame can provide information about course content, dates, locations and other relevant programme details.

Visual responses

Answers can be complemented by course information, images and other visual elements directly on the display.

Physical information kiosk

The system is designed as a freestanding installation with integrated display, camera, microphone and speakers.

Conversational discovery

Instead of only searching for a known course, visitors can ask broader questions and discover suitable UniPop offers through conversation.

Interaction concept

A typical interaction starts with visual course content on the display. The visitor can then ask a question without touching the screen.

The microphone captures the spoken question and converts it into text. The local AI interprets the request and searches the available UniPop information for the most relevant answer.

The result is then converted back into speech and played through the kiosk speakers. At the same time, the display can show the related course, location, dates, imagery or additional recommendations.

Technology

AuraFrame combines several local AI and hardware components into a single interactive system.

A locally running large language model provides the conversational intelligence and is being adapted around UniPop-specific content. Speech recognition converts questions captured by the microphone into text, while text-to-speech generates the spoken response.

The application then coordinates the AI response with the visual interface, allowing information, course imagery and additional details to appear on screen at the same time as the spoken answer.

Running the main AI components locally also makes it possible to design the system around controlled data sources and a dedicated UniPop knowledge environment rather than relying entirely on a generic external chatbot service.
